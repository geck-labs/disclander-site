Band

The mixer: one channel for each seat of this tune's band, and the master that everything passes through. On a channel, pick the instrument (or nobody), slide the fader for how loud it is, press M to mute it (silence this one) or S to solo it (silence all the others, to hear what it is doing). The notes number writes that channel's notes afresh (0 is the tune as built; the dice picks one). Instrument, fader and notes change on the next bar and every tune keeps its own; mute and solo stay as you switch tunes and are not part of the settings you send back.

Inside the instruments

Fine settings of how five of the instruments are built. They are small changes, and they are the same for every tune that uses the instrument. Each line says what to listen for.
