Band

Who sits in each seat of this tune's band, in this style. Pick another instrument for a seat, or nobody, and set how loud that seat is. It changes on the next bar. Every tune keeps its own band for slow and for fast, and the chase has one band whatever the tune.

Inside the instruments

Fine settings of how five of the instruments are built. They are small changes, and they are the same for every tune that uses the instrument. Each line says what to listen for.

Settings to send back

One band plays without stopping, writing the music a bar at a time. There are no songs: it never begins again and never ends. Pick another tune or another style and the band turns on the next bar, in the middle of things, its speed sliding over the bars after. Into the fast style the beat drops in: the band thins out over the lead-in, falls silent for a beat, and everything lands at once; while it stays fast it breaks down and drops again now and then, every other time with the bass landing a bar and a half late. Fast, everything but the drums dips a little as each kick lands (the Pumping knob under Mix; 0 turns it off). The style "fast: the chase" is a fast tune of its own, not a slow tune made to run: whichever tune is picked, the beat drops into the same chase, and going back to slow returns to the tune picked. It is built on one track: a steady 116 beats a minute, the kick drum on beats one and three and once more just before the bar ends, the snare on two and four, a heavy bass on one low note that steps up for the middle of each bar and back, short chords between the beats. The upright lines in the picture are the bars. When something sounds right, copy the settings and send them over, and they become the game's own. The settings text lists every band and instrument you have changed, for all the tunes.
