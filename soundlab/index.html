One band plays without stopping, writing the music a bar at a time. There are no songs: it never begins again and never ends. Pick another tune or the other style and the band turns on the next bar, in the middle of things, its speed sliding over the bars after. The upright lines in the picture are the bars. When something sounds right, copy the settings and send them over, and they become the game's own.
