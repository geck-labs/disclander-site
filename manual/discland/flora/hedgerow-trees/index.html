Hedgerow trees

Old trees left to grow tall out of the hedges, about twice the size of a tree in the open land.

Where they grow

Section titled “Where they grow”
Hedge Trees
Round a great field, meadow or pasture Oaks: about one in three 30 m stretches has one, never near a gate or a corner
Along a road An oak or an ash now and then

On a hollow way, the trees on its banks meet overhead.

Through the year

Section titled “Through the year”

They leaf, turn and stand bare like the other broad-leaved trees.
